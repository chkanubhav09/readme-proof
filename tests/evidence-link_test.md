# Evidence link

## Passing claim evidence
Open the app. In the Claims list, find a claim whose badge says passed, and click its "Detail" button (the button labeled Detail - do NOT click any Verify button, that starts a new run). Verify the Run detail panel shows a link to the claim's evidence.
